The challenge

1

Load the provided dataset (Notebook Section 4) and try at least two models or hyperparameter settings.

2

Log every attempt as its own run — params, metrics and at least one artifact.

3

Pick the best run and log the model with a signature and input example.

4

Register it, write a two-line description, and assign the @champion alias.

Deliverable

A screenshot of your run comparison in the Tracking UI, plus your registered model's name and alias.

Ask questions freely — we'll circulate and help debug live.
